PRIVACY POLICY — LEXTOWN-01 (playlextown.com)

Effective July 7, 2026

LEXTOWN-01 is a free, open-source multiplayer game. It is designed to work without accounts, cookies, or tracking, and this policy describes the small amount of data that passes through it.

WHAT WE PROCESS

  • Player name — whatever name you type (or the random name assigned to you). It is visible to everyone in the world.
  • Position and movement — your avatar's location is relayed to other connected players in real time so they can see you.
  • Chat messages — relayed to everyone connected. Chat is public and unmoderated; do not share personal information.
  • Mission high scores — if you finish the City Hall mission, your chosen player name and completion time are added to the public high-score board. That board is the one thing that persists on the server: the fastest 50 times are kept until displaced by faster ones. Use a name you're happy to see on a leaderboard.

Everything else lives in server memory only while you are connected and is discarded when you disconnect. There is no database, no account system, and no chat history — a message that has scrolled away is gone.

WHAT WE STORE ON YOUR DEVICE

A few localStorage values: a flag that remembers you've dismissed the tutorial, the player name you chose, your sound on/off preference, and your mission progress (best time + whether you've unlocked the helicopter). No advertising, no fingerprinting.

ANALYTICS

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate usage (page views, rough geography, device type). GA sets cookies/identifiers on your browser and processes data under Google's privacy policy. We do not use advertising features, do not sell data, and only look at aggregate numbers. You can block it with any content blocker or Google's opt-out add-on — the game works identically without it.

SERVER LOGS

Like nearly every website, the server keeps short-lived operational logs (connections, disconnections, session lengths, error reports, frame-rate samples, and gameplay counts like darts fired) that can include IP addresses. Chat content is never logged. These logs are used only to keep the service running, find bugs, and deal with abuse; they are automatically deleted after about two weeks. The game is hosted on Amazon Web Services in the United States, whose infrastructure applies its own standard logging.

CHILDREN

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. There is nothing to sign up for.

THIRD PARTIES

Google Analytics (above) and our hosting provider (AWS). No data is sold or shared with anyone else. The complete source code is public at github.com/paul-codes-1/lextown, so you can verify all of this yourself.

CHANGES & CONTACT

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions or concerns: open an issue at github.com/paul-codes-1/lextown/issues.
