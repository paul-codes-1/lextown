                    LEXTOWN-01 · HOW TO PLAY

                    A multiplayer mini-Lexington — downtown to the horse farms. Everyone on this page shares one world. Seven timed missions with global leaderboards; the gold ★ markers on your HUD point the way. Start at the gold ring by City Hall.

                    CALL SIGN

                    shown over your head + in chat · letters, numbers, - _
                    a room code is a code, not a password — share the link and friends join your own Lexington

                    ON FOOT

                    WASDwalk (arrows work too) · Shift run Spacejump — hold it in mid-air to fly the jetpack; watch the fuel gauge, it refills on the ground. You can land on rooftops. Cfirst-person / third-person camera (scrolling all the way in works too) Drag / wheelorbit / zoom the camera

                    CARS

                    Eenter or exit the nearest car (any car — traffic or parked) W/S · A/Ddrive / brake · steer — tops out around 108 km/h Rcar radio — BIG BLUE RADIO / NEWS 630 / 98.5 THE CAT / TRACKSIDE 1450 / off (or tap the dial chip) Enear a friend who's driving: ride shotgun — they drive, you watch the city roll by. E hops out.

                    RIDE THE BUS

                    Ethe LexTown Loop bus circles downtown on a real schedule (Main → MLK → Vine → Broadway). Wait at a marked stop — VICTORIAN SQUARE, PHOENIX PARK, LIBRARY, or TRANSIT CENTER — and the HUD counts down its arrival. When the doors open, E boards; E again hops out at the curb. On boardyou ride to the next stop hands-free — everyone sees the same bus in the same place. R works the radio, C switches camera.

                    MISSION: THE RIBBON CUTTING

                    Eat the gold ring by City Hall (E Main St) — the mayor is dedicating "a horse statue" and the news chopper won't stop buzzing her. Take the ceremonial RPG and shoot it down (holding it aims in first person — look up!). Scorefastest takedown wins — there's a global high-score board (SIM menu → SCORES). Replayable any time, and don't worry: the pilot has a parachute. Unlockbeating it once unlocks the chopper on this device — and opens the City Hall door for the next mission

                    MISSION 2: SNOW EMERGENCY

                    Eat the City Hall door (unlocked by beating THE RIBBON CUTTING) — the mayor needs downtown plowed before the storm wins Spaceraises / lowers the plow blade. Blade down on snow clears it; blade down on bare pavement grinds up the road and costs you time penalties Goalclear the five glowing streets. Timed, with its own global board. And whatever you do, don't plow the mayor's street — half of reddit is camped out there.

                    MISSION 3: THE DATA CENTER

                    Eat the teal ring in Phoenix Park — the mayor suspects a councilman is up to something. Tail him up Limestone (grab a car), eavesdrop behind Al's Bar, then beat him to the UK quad. F / clicktakes a photo once you're at the quad — get three shots of the scouting trip without getting close enough to be noticed. Then bring the camera back. The internet does the rest.

                    MISSION 4: HORSEPOWER

                    Eat the green ring in Thoroughbred Park — three thoroughbreds got loose the night before the auction. One is in that park pretending to be a statue. Wranglewalk up slow (run at a horse and the horse wins), press E for the lead rope. A calm horse follows you — and if you get in a car, the horse gets in the car. Deliver all three to the green ring at Elmendorf Farm, past New Circle.

                    MISSION 5: DEADLINE

                    Eat the gold ring on Main St outside The Block newsroom — the six o'clock needs art. Grab a car and drive five downtown checkpoints against a 180-second clock. Rings only bank while you're in a car. GHOSTyour best DEADLINE run replays as a translucent ghost car — race yourself, and read the AHEAD/BEHIND split at every checkpoint. Toggle it with GHOST in the SIM menu.

                    MISSION 6: THE MELT

                    Eat the pink ring at the Crank & Boom stand, W Main by the Distillery District — Lexington's shop is in a national fan-voted ice-cream bracket and the voters haven't tasted a spoonful. Grab a car and make five scoop stops before the cooler melts. Drive niceevery crash sloshes the cooler and melts it 20 seconds faster. Rocky road is a flavor, not a driving style.

                    MISSION 7: TAILGATE COMPLIANCE

                    Eat the blue ring by the Kroger Field lots — new tailgating guidelines: every structure tagged with owner contact info. Eight canopies just appeared. In July. You have four minutes and a clipboard. Tagstand by a canopy to tag it (on foot — clipboards don't work from a car window). Two used deep ground stakes near tree roots; stand there until the stakes come out.

                    MISSION 8: LOOSE IN THE PADDOCK

                    Eat the amber ring at Elmendorf Farm (north, past New Circle) — three foals jumped the rail before the Keeneland yearling sale. Draw the blaster (G) and settle each one with a calming dart; a settled foal trots itself to the pen. Aim ahead of a moving foal.

                    MISSION 9: AIR MAIL

                    Eat the violet ring downtown (right by spawn) — the street's a parking lot and the mail truck rolls at six, so the postmaster straps you into the jetpack (Space to climb, ease off to glide). Fly the day's airmail rooftop to rooftop. Land on the padsthe route alternates violet air-rings (fly through) with rooftop pads (set down to bank the stop and refuel — fuel only fills on the ground). Climb Victorian-low roofs up to the Central Bank Tower and finish on Big Blue. Beat the 3-minute clock.

                    THE NEWS CHOPPER

                    Eon Big Blue's rooftop helipad — fly the LEXINGTON KY NEWS chopper (one pilot at a time; jetpack up there). Locked until you beat THE RIBBON CUTTING. W/S · A/Dfly / turn · Space climb · Shift descend · E lands (or bails out — the chopper crashes) F / clickhold for the water cannon — the jet shoves players around (no damage, no opt-in needed) RPGwhile the chopper is up, RPG crates glow around downtown. Grab one and F fires (holding it locks you into first-person aim) — three hits bring it down, and it respawns on Big Blue. Rockets only hurt the helicopter.

                    NERF TAG (OPT-IN)

                    Gdraw / holster your nerf blaster — drawing it opts you into PvP; holstered players can't be hit F / clickfire a foam dart where you're looking. A hit freezes the target in an ice cube for 4 seconds. That's it — no health, no scores, just tag.
