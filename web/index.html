      LEXTOWN-01 · HOW TO PLAY

      A multiplayer mini-downtown-Lexington. Everyone on this page shares one world.

      CALL SIGN

      shown over your head + in chat · letters, numbers, - _

      ON FOOT

      WASDwalk (arrows work too) · Shift run Spacejump — hold it in mid-air to fly the jetpack; watch the fuel gauge, it refills on the ground. You can land on rooftops. Cfirst-person / third-person camera (scrolling all the way in works too) Drag / wheelorbit / zoom the camera

      CARS

      Eenter or exit the nearest car (any car — traffic or parked) W/S · A/Ddrive / brake · steer — tops out around 108 km/h

      MISSION: THE RIBBON CUTTING

      Eat the gold ring by City Hall (E Main St) — the mayor is dedicating "a horse statue" and the news chopper won't stop buzzing her. Take the ceremonial RPG and shoot it down (holding it aims in first person — look up!). Scorefastest takedown wins — there's a global high-score board (SIM menu → SCORES). Replayable any time, and don't worry: the pilot has a parachute. Unlockbeating it once unlocks the chopper on this device — and opens the City Hall door for the next mission

      MISSION 2: SNOW EMERGENCY

      Eat the City Hall door (unlocked by beating THE RIBBON CUTTING) — the mayor needs downtown plowed before the storm wins Spaceraises / lowers the plow blade. Blade down on snow clears it; blade down on bare pavement grinds up the road and costs you time penalties Goalclear the five glowing streets. Timed, with its own global board. And whatever you do, don't plow the mayor's street — half of reddit is camped out there.

      THE NEWS CHOPPER

      Eon Big Blue's rooftop helipad — fly the LEXINGTON KY NEWS chopper (one pilot at a time; jetpack up there). Locked until you beat THE RIBBON CUTTING. W/S · A/Dfly / turn · Space climb · Shift descend · E lands (or bails out — the chopper crashes) F / clickhold for the water cannon — the jet shoves players around (no damage, no opt-in needed) RPGwhile the chopper is up, RPG crates glow around downtown. Grab one and F fires (holding it locks you into first-person aim) — three hits bring it down, and it respawns on Big Blue. Rockets only hurt the helicopter.

      NERF TAG (OPT-IN)

      Gdraw / holster your nerf blaster — drawing it opts you into PvP; holstered players can't be hit F / clickfire a foam dart where you're looking. A hit freezes the target in an ice cube for 4 seconds. That's it — no health, no scores, just tag.
